What are spiritual gifts?

Spiritual gifts are abilities God gives to every believer through the Holy Spirit to build up the church and serve others. They are not the same as natural talents — they are empowered by the Spirit and meant for ministry. The Bible lists many of them in Romans 12, 1 Corinthians 12, and Ephesians 4.

This assessment uses the widely-used LifeWay Spiritual Gifts Survey: 80 short statements you answer honestly (there are no wrong answers — this is not a test). Your answers reveal the gifts where you appear strongest, so you can use them in service to Christ and His body.

How to take the survey

  1. Answer each of the 80 statements on a scale of 1–5.
  2. Answer honestly and quickly — your first reaction is usually best.
  3. Don't overthink any single item, and don't skip any.
  4. There are no right or wrong answers. This is not a test.
  5. When finished, your results will rank your 16 gifts with a clear chart.
